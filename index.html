How it works

Three modes

  • Find it — you get a name (or a clue) and tap the structure on the body. Drag to turn it, pinch or scroll to zoom. Two misses and it lights up — tap it to move on.
  • Name it — a structure glows and you pick its name from four. This is the shape her tests use ("What is the name of structure B?"). Deep muscles are asked here only, with the body x-rayed.
  • Explore — no questions. Tap anything to see what it is.

"Her list"

Every bone and muscle that is an answer somewhere in the HS2 Module 2 quiz bank (471 questions, read by hand). It is about 30 bones, 10 landmarks on the femur and hip bone, and 30 muscles — not 200. Clues marked her quiz are paraphrased from her own questions. "Everything" adds the structures a first-year course usually expects on top.

What the numbers mean

The ring on each deck is how many of her list you have locked: answered right first time, twice in a row. A miss unlocks it. Rounds deal what you have not locked first. Nothing is due, nothing expires, nothing leaves this browser.

Left and right

Either side counts. The body faces you, so its left is on your right — exactly like her figures.
